AutoAudit-360

End-to-End Trustworthy AI Audit and Regulatory Compliance Suite

Upload a dataset, and AutoAudit-360 trains a baseline model, tests it for bias, explainability, robustness and privacy risk, and gives you a Trust Score, a compliance readiness checklist, and a downloadable PDF report.

The problem

AI systems now help decide who gets a loan, a job or a service. Models can be accurate overall and still treat groups differently, hide how they decide, break under slightly noisy inputs, or be trained on personal data that should never have been collected. Regulations such as the EU AI Act and India's DPDP Act expect organisations to check for these risks, but doing it by hand is slow and inconsistent. AutoAudit-360 turns those checks into one repeatable workflow.

Five audit modules

Fairness

Measures demographic parity and equalized odds gaps between groups, plus accuracy per group, using Fairlearn.

Explainability

Uses SHAP to show which features drive predictions, and flags when a sensitive attribute is a top driver.

Robustness

Adds increasing Gaussian noise to the test data and tracks how much accuracy drops.

Privacy

Scans column names and values for Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, emails and other likely personal data.

Compliance

Combines all results into a checklist mapped to regulations, and a weighted 0 to 100 Trust Score.

PDF report

Exports the scores, charts, checklist and limitations as a report you can attach to your records.

How it works

  1. UploadChoose the sample dataset or upload a CSV, then pick the target and sensitive attribute.
  2. AuditA baseline Random Forest is trained and the four audit modules run on it.
  3. ScoreEach module gets a 0 to 100 score, combined into one Trust Score.
  4. ReportReview the checklist and download the PDF.

Demo findings: UCI Adult dataset

We audited a random sample of 5,000 rows of the UCI Adult income dataset, predicting whether income exceeds $50K, with sex as the sensitive attribute. These numbers come from one run of the default settings and will vary slightly between runs.

90Trust Score (out of 100)
85.6%Model accuracy
0.166Demographic parity gap (threshold 0.10)
0.069Equalized odds gap

The model is biased even with a high score

The parity gap of 0.166 means the model predicts the higher income class noticeably more often for one sex than the other, so the bias check fails. Because a failed check caps the rating at "Needs attention", a high Trust Score cannot hide a failed fairness check.

Fairness metrics can disagree

The equalized odds gap (0.069) is under the threshold while the parity gap is not. Passing one fairness metric does not mean a model is fair, so the tool reports both.

Proxy discrimination

Sex is not among the model's top three drivers, yet the gap remains. The top drivers include marital status and relationship features, which correlate strongly with sex. Removing a sensitive column does not remove the bias.

Robust and privacy-clean

Accuracy held up well under added noise (robustness score 99), and the dataset contained no direct personal identifiers.

Limitations

  • The tool audits a baseline model it trains itself, not a separately trained production model.
  • Scores, weights and thresholds are adjustable heuristics, not values defined by any regulation.
  • Privacy screening is pattern-based and cannot detect names or free-text addresses by value.
  • Human oversight and documentation cannot be verified from data, so they are marked "Not assessed".
  • AutoAudit-360 is a compliance readiness screen, not a legal opinion or a certification.
